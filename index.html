How to connect
  1. On your phone, join the WiFi network Hilton Honors Meeting Network.
  2. Open any web browser — the Hilton sign-in page opens automatically.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of that page.
  4. Tap “I have a promo code.”
  5. Enter the code Advance and connect.
Trouble connecting? The front desk or a TRMA staffer at registration can help.

Add to Home Screen

Save this guide as an app icon on your phone — it opens full-screen, and it's one tap away all conference.

iPhone & iPadSafari
  1. In Safari, tap the Share button (bottom of the screen).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top-right. The TRMA icon lands on your home screen.
AndroidChrome
  1. In Chrome, tap the menu (top-right).
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Tap Add / Install. The TRMA icon lands on your home screen.
Once added, it launches full-screen like a native app — and your private notes stay saved right on your phone.
